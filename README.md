# PROTEENSNAX 🚀

## Real Protein for a Stronger Tomorrow

PROTEENSNAX is an entrepreneurship project developed as part of the Entrepreneurship Module at IBI.

Our idea focuses on providing affordable, tasty and protein-rich food products with Indian flavours and accessible nutrition.

## 🎯 Project Objective

To understand how a business can be developed from scratch — from identifying a problem and developing a product to understanding customers, pricing, marketing and business execution.
